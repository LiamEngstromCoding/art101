Results

Task 1: Pseudocode an everyday task

Pseudocode for cooking toast: 

-Retrieve bread and butter from fridge
-Retrieve plate and knife- leave on counter near toaster
-Put bread in toaster
-Toast bread
-Remove toasted bread from toaster
-Place toasted bread on plate
-Spread butter on toasted bread with knife
-Put away bread, butter, and knife

Task 2: Pseudocode a simple computer game

Rock-Paper-Scissors 

-Assign players to either designations “Player 1” and “Player 2”
-When round starts, provide 3 available the inputs (rock, paper, scissors) for the first player
	-Prompt them to select an input
-Once input is given by “Player 1”, Provide the inputs again and prompt the second player to choose an input.
-After both players have selected inputs, decide if a player has reached a winning instance. 
	-Winning instances: 
		-Rock beats scissors
		-Scissors beats paper
		-Paper beats rock
	-If both players play the same input, the round is a draw, and no one gets points. The round starts over
-The player that has reached the winning instance gains a point
	-First player to reach two points wins the game
